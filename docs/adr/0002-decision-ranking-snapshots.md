# Superseded — Preserve Ranking snapshots with Next Opportunity decisions

**Status:** Superseded by ADR-0004. Ranking and Next Opportunity decisions are no longer canonical product concepts.

Opportunity Atlas records the Ranking and weights with each Next Opportunity decision, while retaining a separately calculated live Ranking within the same Iteration. This preserves why a team made a decision at the time without concealing later changes in that reassessment cycle.
