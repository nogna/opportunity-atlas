# Superseded — Version the decision artifact through Iterations

**Status:** Superseded by ADR-0004. Opportunity Maps are living artifacts and are never locked by a decision.

Opportunity Atlas keeps one long-lived editable Iteration during exploration and evaluation, then sets it read-only when the team records its Next Opportunity decision. A new Iteration copies that completed artifact for a later reassessment, preserving prior reasoning without turning every in-progress change into a version.
