HOW OPPORTUNITY ATLAS WORKS

Start with the possibilities. Learn your way forward.

  1. Map opportunities. Add or develop Islands until the team has a shared view of what could be worth exploring.
  2. Shape an Island. Use its Chart Room to clarify the workflow, AI-enabled change, outcome, evidence, and current values.
  3. Plan an Expedition. Select the prepared Islands worth learning about now and give each a next learning action.

Scouting notes and Map changes are available when they help; they are not required to begin.
