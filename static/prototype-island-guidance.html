Workflow sketch Optional · clarify the handoff

Readiness & data Open when moving toward delivery
Check ticket-text access, quality, consistency, retention constraints, and the integrations needed to place a reviewable signal in the lead’s existing work.
Safeguards & risk Open early for sensitive work
Name harms, oversight, escalation, and an accountable owner. For this Island, signals remain advisory and a lead reviews every action.

Who reviews the signal? Who can override it? What changes in the lead’s routine, and what support will they need?
